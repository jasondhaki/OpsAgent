# Returns and damaged items

FICTIONAL DEMO DATA.

## Damaged or wrong item
If your bag arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, reply with your order number and a
photo within 3 days. The owner will contact you personally.

ব্যাগ ছেঁড়া বা ভুল পণ্য এলে ৩ দিনের মধ্যে অর্ডার নম্বর ও ছবি পাঠান।

## Exchange
Exchanges are possible for unused items. The owner confirms each case.

## Refunds
Refunds are decided by the owner case by case.
